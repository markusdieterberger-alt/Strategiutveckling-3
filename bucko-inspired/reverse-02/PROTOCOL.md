# BUCKO REVERSE 02 — frozen protocol, 18 October 2026

This protocol is written before any REVERSE 02 economic outcomes are calculated. This is a post-hoc exploratory experiment, not independent validation. No signal, score, pivot, FVG, supply/demand, session or regime calculation is changed. RECON 04 and REVERSE 01 files remain read-only.

## Sources and scope
Reuse the verified REVERSE 01 reproducible package and its embedded RECON 04 data, original reference code, cached causal signal traces, calendar roll and holiday blackout. Preserve all legacy contract resets/warmup and data limitations. Master and legacy are reported separately. No tick delta or external data purchase. 1 MNQ = USD 2/point; tick 0.25; commission USD 1 round turn. Original signal scores/types refer to the approved SOURCE direction, even after reversal.

## A. Exact mathematical mirror
Create a new original-direction 1:1 reference book, independent of previously observed 1.5/2/3R books. On a confirmed source signal, original SL is the known latest pivot plus/minus one tick. At the next available open O, require valid distance d to that original stop. Set common boundaries O-d and O+d. The fixed-opportunity mirror reverses direction and exchanges SL/TP roles at these identical boundaries. Record forced same-exit gross negation separately from independently evaluated brackets on these fixed entry opportunities (which may overlap). Count unique first-boundary hits, OHLC both-touch ambiguity, gaps, penetration, timing and net-sign exceptions. Only the separately simulated one-position book is executable. Mathematical forced inversion is not a trading fill.

## B. Two separate reversed families
S = STRUCT_NEW_SIDE: new-side latest confirmed pivot, one-tick buffer; validate at signal close and next open. Preserve RECON 04 actual slipped entry and target construction: target = slipped entry + direction*RR*distance(slipped entry, stop), rounded outward to tick. No older convenient pivot search.
M = MIRROR_ORIGINAL_DISTANCE: validate the original-direction structural SL; d = distance from observable next open to that SL. Reverse direction; stop O-direction*d, target O+direction*RR*d, rounded outward. These common-open levels implement the requested symmetric-distance alternative. The reference distance is original 1:1, not a selected prior winning RR. Levels use no entry-bar H/L/C. Costs apply to cash flow, not to M geometry.
RR = [1, 0.75, 0.50, 0.33, 0.20, 0.10], all reported. Independent candidate outcomes are precomputed for every eligible source signal, including those skipped by another book. Account sizing never inherits a one-contract book's selection.
Fill scenarios: BASE1 (one adverse tick each side); SLIP2 (two ticks each side); DELAY60 (BASE1, no TP in entry bar); DELAY60_SLIP2 (both restrictions). Stop always active. Targets require one-tick penetration and receive no favorable gap improvement. Stop wins a both-touch OHLC bar. Stop gaps fill at worse observed open. Opposite signals ignored while occupied; one position; next observed 1m-open entry after confirmed signal only. Missing bars use inherited next observed open gap exit; crossing a session/curfew while held is a DATA_UNRESOLVED state in lifecycle simulation, not a fabricated fill or a PASS. Such paths are reported separately, paid fees remain spent and no payout is assigned.

## Integer sizing and daily policies
Challenge risk budgets USD [100,200,300,400,500,575], including full planned commission and slippage. Funded risk=min(challenge risk,200), frozen defensive policy. q=floor(min(risk,remaining DLL-25,remaining MLL-25)/one-contract planned stop loss including costs), capped by phase max position. q<1 means skip, not fractional contracts. No future target or outcome influences q. Continuous adverse excursions are bounded by the active stop; gap overshoots can exceed limits and are handled explicitly. USD25 buffer is not a guarantee against gaps.
P0: no voluntary daily stop. P300: stop opening after realized daily profit>=300 or loss<=-300. P600: profit>=600 or loss<=-300. Voluntary stops checked after exits and can overshoot; DLL and MLL checked as actual protective state rules. A gap crossing both records both flags, MLL is terminal. Daily stop resets next session.
